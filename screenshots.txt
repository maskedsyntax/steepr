Screenshot Strategy

The first 3 screenshots should sell the core promise immediately:

Steepr is not just a timer. It is a calm, guided tea ritual app that helps people brew better tea without thinking about timing.

The App Store screenshots should make the user feel:

"I want my tea routine to feel this simple."

Core Positioning

Primary promise:
Better tea, timed beautifully.

Supporting promise:
Steepr guides every part of your brew, from water cooling to steeping, with clean timers and gentle reminders.

Emotional angle:
A calmer, more intentional tea ritual.

Functional angle:
Multi-step tea timers, saved profiles, notifications, watch support, widgets, and brew history.

Recommended Screenshot Set

Use 6 to 8 screenshots total.

Screenshot 1: Hero Hook

Goal:
Instantly communicate the app's value.

Visual:
Show the main active timer screen with a beautiful tea profile running. Large timer, current step, calm UI.

Headline options:
Never Oversteep Again
Brew Better Tea
Your Tea, Timed Perfectly

Subtext:
Guided timers for every step of your tea ritual.

Best headline:
Never Oversteep Again

Why:
It solves a clear pain immediately.

Screenshot 2: Multi-Step Brewing

Goal:
Show that this is more than a basic countdown timer.

Visual:
Show a tea profile with ordered steps like:
Boil Water
Cool to 175F
Steep
Enjoy

Headline:
Every Step, Guided

Subtext:
Create tea profiles with multiple timed steps.

Why:
This is the product's key differentiator.

Screenshot 3: Calm Active Timer

Goal:
Sell the experience and emotional benefit.

Visual:
Full-screen brew timer with clean typography, soft colors, and minimal controls.

Headline:
A Calmer Way to Brew

Subtext:
Clear countdowns, gentle transitions, no clutter.

Why:
This helps the app feel premium, not utilitarian.

Screenshot 4: Saved Tea Profiles

Goal:
Show repeat usage and personalization.

Visual:
Library/profile screen with teas like Green Tea, Earl Grey, Oolong, Matcha, Custom Brew.

Headline:
Save Your Favorites

Subtext:
Keep perfect timings for every tea you love.

Why:
Shows long-term value.

Screenshot 5: Notifications

Goal:
Show practical reliability.

Visual:
Mock notification or in-app transition showing a completed step.

Headline:
Know Exactly When It's Ready

Subtext:
Get notified when each step is complete.

Why:
Reassures users the app will actually help.

Screenshot 6: Widgets / Live Activity

Goal:
Show convenience.

Visual:
Lock screen or home screen widget/live activity style presentation.

Headline:
Track Your Brew Anywhere

Subtext:
Keep an eye on your timer without opening the app.

Why:
Strong modern iOS value.

Screenshot 7: Apple Watch

Goal:
Sell ecosystem polish.

Visual:
Watch timer screen beside phone.

Headline:
On Your Wrist, Too

Subtext:
Start and follow your brew from Apple Watch.

Why:
Good for perceived quality and differentiation.

Screenshot 8: Brew History / Journal

Goal:
Show depth without making it feel complex.

Visual:
History screen with recent brews or notes.

Headline:
Remember What Worked

Subtext:
Track your favorite brews and refine your ritual.

Why:
Appeals to tea enthusiasts.

First Three Screenshot Flow

The first three should work as a mini sales funnel:

1. Never Oversteep Again
Pain point and immediate promise.

2. Every Step, Guided
Differentiator and practical value.

3. A Calmer Way to Brew
Emotional payoff and brand feel.

Together they say:
Steepr helps you brew better tea, guides the full process, and makes the experience peaceful.

Visual Direction

Use a calm premium visual language:

Colors:
warm cream, soft green, deep charcoal, tea amber, muted clay.

Typography:
Large, elegant, readable. Avoid overly playful fonts.

Layout:
Use big headlines at the top, app UI in the center, small supporting text at the bottom.

Composition:
The app screen should be large enough to read. Do not overcrowd.

Mood:
Minimal, warm, focused, intentional.

Avoid:
Busy backgrounds, too much text, generic phone mockups, feature dumping, harsh gradients.

Copy Style

Keep every screenshot short.

Good:
Never Oversteep Again
Every Step, Guided
Save Your Favorites
Track Your Brew Anywhere

Avoid:
Create custom tea profiles with multiple steps and receive notifications

Reason:
Users scan screenshots in seconds.

App Store vs Play Store

For App Store:
Use iPhone-first screenshots, plus optional Apple Watch-focused screenshot if supported.
Lean into polish, calmness, widgets, Live Activities, Apple Watch.

For Play Store:
If the app is not actually on Android, do not prepare Play Store screenshots yet.
If an Android version is planned, keep the same story but remove Apple-specific features like Apple Watch, Live Activities, and iOS widgets unless Android equivalents exist.

Play Store version would be:

1. Never Oversteep Again
2. Every Step, Guided
3. A Calmer Way to Brew
4. Save Your Favorites
5. Know When It's Ready
6. Track Every Brew
