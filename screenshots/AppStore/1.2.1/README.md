# Steepr 1.2.1 App Store screenshots

The numbered filenames define the App Store order.

1. `01-perfect-steep.png` — designed hero composition with the authentic 1.2.1 timer UI
2. `02-brew-from-your-wrist.png` — Apple Watch differentiator with compact, bandless device cases
3. `03-tea-library.png` — built-in presets and favorites
4. `04-visible-everywhere.png` — Live Activities and widgets
5. `05-guided-infusions.png` — Pro guided re-steeps
6. `06-private-by-design.png` — privacy positioning
7. `07-steepr-pro.png` — purchase screen, intentionally last

The 1.2 screenshots showing a caffeine total, the old timer's “Next” step, and
the caffeine-led journal are intentionally excluded because they do not match
the 1.2.1 product.

The editable source for screenshots 1 and 2 is in `design/index.html`. Its two
background textures were generated without text or devices; all typography,
device cases, and real app UI are composed deterministically in HTML/CSS.
