## Common Questions

### How do I create a custom tea?

Open the Library tab, choose to add a tea, then set the name, steep time, temperature, notes, color, and icon.

### Why can I only create a few custom teas?

The free version includes a small number of custom teas. Steepr Pro unlocks unlimited custom teas.

### How do I use Steepr on Apple Watch?

Favorite teas on iPhone appear on Apple Watch. Open Steepr on Apple Watch to start a favorite tea, control the timer, and receive haptic alerts. Supported watch faces can show brew progress in a complication.

### Can I start a brew with Siri or a Shortcut?

Yes. Steepr provides App Intents, so you can start a tea by voice with Siri or from the Shortcuts app without opening Steepr.

### How do guided re-steeps work?

After an infusion finishes, Steepr offers the next one with its timing already adjusted, so oolong, pu-erh, and gongfu sessions stay on track. Guided re-steeps are part of Steepr Pro.

### Where does the daily caffeine total come from?

It is estimated from the teas you brewed today and shown on the Brew tab. It is a guide for your own reference, not a medical measurement.

### Do widgets, Live Activities, and the Dynamic Island follow the active timer?

Yes. Widgets, Live Activities, and the Dynamic Island use a local shared timer snapshot so they show the current brew state.

### Does Steepr sync with iCloud?

Steepr Pro includes iCloud sync for teas, preferences, and brew history. Active timers remain local-only.

### How do I restore Steepr Pro?

Open Settings in Steepr and use Restore purchases. Purchases are restored through your Apple ID, and Pro is covered by Family Sharing.

### How do I contact support?

Email support@maskedsyntax.com with your device model, iOS or watchOS version, and a short description of the issue.
