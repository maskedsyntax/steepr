## Common Questions

### How do I create a custom tea?

Open the Library tab, choose to add a tea, then set the name, steep time, temperature, notes, color, and icon.

### Why can I only create a few custom teas?

The free version includes a small number of custom teas. Steepr Pro unlocks unlimited custom teas.

### How do I use Steepr on Apple Watch?

Favorite teas on iPhone appear on Apple Watch. Open Steepr on Apple Watch to start a favorite tea, control the timer, and receive haptic alerts.

### Do widgets and Live Activities sync with the active timer?

Yes. Widgets and Live Activities use a local shared timer snapshot so they can show the current brew state.

### Does Steepr sync with iCloud?

Steepr Pro includes iCloud sync for teas, preferences, and brew history. Active timers remain local-only.

### How do I restore Steepr Pro?

Open Settings in Steepr and use Restore purchases. Purchases are restored through your Apple ID.

### How do I contact support?

Email support@maskedsyntax.com with your device model, iOS or watchOS version, and a short description of the issue.
