# Privacy Policy

Effective date: To be updated before launch.

Steepr is designed to collect as little information as possible.

## Data Steepr Stores

Steepr stores the data needed to run the app:

- Tea profiles you create or edit.
- Favorite teas.
- Brewing preferences.
- Brew history.
- Active timer state.
- Purchase entitlement state for Steepr Pro.

## Where Data Is Stored

By default, Steepr stores app data locally on your device.

If you use Steepr Pro and iCloud sync is enabled, Steepr syncs teas, preferences, and brew history using Apple CloudKit in your private iCloud database.

Active timer state is local-only and is not synced through iCloud.

## Data Steepr Does Not Collect

Steepr does not collect:

- Analytics or tracking data.
- Advertising identifiers.
- Location.
- Contacts.
- Health data.
- Account credentials.
- Personal notes outside the tea notes you choose to enter.

## Notifications

If you allow notifications, Steepr uses them only for tea timer alerts, including optional pre-completion alerts and brew completion alerts.

## Purchases

Steepr Pro purchases are handled by Apple through StoreKit. Steepr receives purchase entitlement status from Apple, but does not receive your payment information.

## Data Deletion

You can delete custom teas and brew history in the app. Deleting the app removes local app data from the device. If iCloud sync is enabled, data stored in iCloud may remain available to your Apple ID according to Apple's iCloud behavior.
