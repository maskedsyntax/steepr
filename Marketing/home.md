# Steepr

The perfect cup, every time.

Steepr is a calm tea timer for iPhone and Apple Watch. Save your favorite teas, start a brew in seconds, and let Steepr handle the timing while you enjoy the ritual.

## Features

- Built-in tea presets for green, black, oolong, white, herbal, chai, pu-erh, and matcha.
- Custom teas with steep time, temperature, notes, color, and icon.
- Sequential timer controls with pause, resume, cancel, and brew-again actions.
- Lock Screen widgets and Live Activities for the active brew.
- Apple Watch quick-start favorites, active timer controls, haptics, and complications.
- Optional notifications for pre-alerts and completion.

## Steepr Pro

Steepr Pro is a one-time purchase for people who brew often.

- Unlimited custom teas.
- iCloud sync for teas, preferences, and brew history.
- Advanced haptics.
- Additional calm alert sounds.
- Family Sharing support.

## Privacy

No accounts. No ads. No tracking. Tea data stays on your device unless you enable iCloud sync through Steepr Pro, in which case Apple CloudKit stores your data in your private iCloud database.
